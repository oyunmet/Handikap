# World model assets

The bundled models below are downloaded as self-contained glTF Binary (`.glb`) files. Their embedded image maps use the 1K (1024 px) Poly Haven variants.

| --- | --- | --- |
| `rock-wall.glb` | [Rock 07 — Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_07) | CC0 1.0 |
| `wooden-barrel.glb` | [Wooden Barrels 01 — Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_barrels_01) | CC0 1.0 |
| `treasure-chest.glb` | [Treasure Chest — Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/treasure_chest) | CC0 1.0 |
| `ceramic-vase.glb` | [Ceramic Vase 01 — Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/ceramic_vase_01) | CC0 1.0 |

Poly Haven asset license: <https://polyhaven.com/license>.

The armored knight model and its animation clips already existed in `public/models/gece-nobetcisi.glb`; it is reused here and is not represented as a newly sourced Poly Haven or Quaternius download. The rest of the environment uses the scene's procedural, smoothed geometry where no suitable local GLB was available.
