---
name: Campaign record persistence
description: Preserve permanent campaign story unlocks independently of short duel-history lists.
---

Campaign story records are permanent collectibles and must not be inferred only from the recent defeated-opponent list.

**Why:** that history is intentionally bounded for profile size, while an unlocked operation record must remain available in the archive indefinitely.

**How to apply:** store validated story-record IDs separately, grant them only after server-verified operation-final victories, and use recent opponent history only for one-time migration of older profiles.
