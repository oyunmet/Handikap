---
name: War Room camera framing
description: The War Room must remain third-person with the player visible.
---

The War Room always uses third-person framing with the player's back toward the camera; do not restore first-person from legacy preferences. When the player is inside a building, keep the camera and look target inside that building so the interior remains visible. Recover the player to a clear nearby position if they enter blocking geometry.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires a rear third-person view and reported that the indoor camera staying outside house walls hides the interior.

**How to apply:** Preserve third-person as the only player-facing mode; keep the camera at least 2.5 metres away outdoors when clear, and prioritize remaining inside the active building indoors.
