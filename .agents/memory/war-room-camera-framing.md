---
name: War Room camera framing
description: The War Room must remain third-person with the player visible.
---

The War Room always uses third-person framing with the player's back toward the camera; do not restore first-person from legacy preferences or show the player's front. When the player is inside a building, keep the camera and look target inside that building, use closer framing than outdoors, and keep the interior visible. Recover the player to a clear nearby position if they enter blocking geometry.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires a rear third-person view and reports that distant indoor framing and wall occlusion make the house view unreadable.

**How to apply:** Preserve third-person rear framing; keep the camera at least 2.5 metres away outdoors when clear, and shorten the indoor distance while keeping the camera and look target inside the active building.
