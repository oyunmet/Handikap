---
name: War Room mobile controls
description: Portrait mobile placement expectations for the movement joystick and combat HUD.
---

In portrait mobile War Room play, keep the movement joystick fixed in the lower-left thumb-reach area. Keep health, stamina, and weapon details out of that area; place them together in a clear upper-left stack. The fixed joystick is the default, while dynamic positioning remains available only as an explicit choice. Preserve landscape and custom control layouts.

**Why:** The user reported that the joystick appeared too high and requested that it occupy the lower-left health/stamina area, with the status information moved elsewhere.

**How to apply:** Default and migrate older saved preferences to the fixed lower-left stick, while preserving a deliberate dynamic selection in current preferences. Keep safe-area spacing and test both portrait phone sizes and landscape layouts.
